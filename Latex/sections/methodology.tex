\chapter{Methodology}

\section{Research Framing}

\textit{What should go here:} restate the dissertation's central aim in methodological terms. Explain that the project evaluates a supplementary VR learning environment rather than attempting to prove that VR broadly replaces conventional teaching.

\subsection{Research Question}

\textit{What should go here:} present the main research question in its final wording and briefly explain what the ``to what extent'' phrasing means in practice for this study.

\subsection{Hypotheses}

\textit{What should go here:} introduce the formal hypothesis set. A sensible structure is one main hypothesis, followed by bounded sub-hypotheses on usability, conceptual support, guided-to-independent progression, post-session knowledge evidence, and value as a supplement rather than a replacement.

\section{Educational Scope and Learning Outcomes}

\textit{What should go here:} define the educational scope of the study and state the final learning outcomes that the prototype is designed to support. This section should also explain why the outcomes are deliberately narrower than a full architecture course and how they act as the bridge between motivation, design, and evaluation.

\section{Study Design and Rationale}

\textit{What should go here:} describe the study as an exploratory, formative evaluation of a focused educational prototype. Explain why this kind of design is appropriate for the project, what kinds of claims it can support, and what kinds of claims it cannot support.

\section{Participants}

\textit{What should go here:} describe the intended participant profile, recruitment source, inclusion boundaries, and any relevant background characteristics that matter for interpretation.

\section{Procedure}

\textit{What should go here:} give the participant-study flow in order: consent, background questions, tailored refresher, optional baseline probe, VR session, post-session questionnaire and knowledge check, and researcher notes.

\section{Instruments and Data Capture}

\textit{What should go here:} describe each evidence source separately. This should cover the background form, the short baseline subset, the VR session itself, post-session Likert items, the knowledge check, open-ended feedback, researcher observation notes, and session metadata such as attempted modes or instruction types.

\section{Analysis Plan}

\textit{What should go here:} explain how the evidence will be analysed. This should include descriptive quantitative summaries, baseline-versus-post comparisons where appropriate, thematic coding of open-ended responses and observation notes, and triangulation across the different evidence sources.

\section{Ethical Considerations and Study Boundaries}

\textit{What should go here:} summarise consent, confidentiality, participant welfare, and the main limits on interpretation. This is also the place to say clearly that the dissertation does not claim long-term retention, broad generalisability, or the replacement of traditional instruction.
